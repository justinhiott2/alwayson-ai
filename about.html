About GearScout

GearScout is an independent product-discovery project focused on practical products for home, outdoors, auto and everyday life.

We aim to explain what matters before you buy: intended use, key specifications, trade-offs, warranty, return terms and total cost.

We do not claim hands-on testing unless a guide explicitly says a product was tested and describes how.

Editorial standards

  • Disclose affiliate relationships clearly.
  • Do not publish fake reviews, ratings, test results or scarcity claims.
  • Check product availability and pricing before publishing.
  • Correct or remove inaccurate and outdated information.
