Backup and privacy

Your profile, imported records, leads and claim notes stay in this browser. Clearing browser data can erase them. Export a backup regularly and keep it somewhere private.

What is and is not automated

  • Automated: local record matching, lead organization, scoring hints, claim checklist generation and backup export.
  • Assisted: searches through official portals, claim preparation and claim status tracking.
  • Not automated: government database access where no public API exists, identity verification, signing legal declarations, bypassing CAPTCHA, and submitting claims without your direct review and authorization.
