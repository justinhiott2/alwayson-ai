6. Nationwide legal review checklist

  • Confirm the state and county where the property is located and current licensing / wholesaling rules.
  • Identify whether you are acting as principal, licensed broker, marketing provider, or another permitted role.
  • Have local counsel review the purchase agreement, assignment / novation language, marketing disclosures and cancellation rights.
  • Verify who may receive compensation and whether any referral, finder, marketing or transaction fee requires a license.
  • Confirm earnest-money amount, due date, refund conditions and named escrow holder in writing.
  • Verify title, liens, taxes, access, zoning, occupancy, disclosures and buyer funding before promising a closing.
  • Never misrepresent ownership, control, seller authorization, buyer funds or the right to assign.
