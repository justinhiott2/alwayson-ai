4. Lead verification and company fit

A lead becomes a potential company claim only when the record identifies the same legal entity and the Treasurer/court/agency confirms its claimant requirements. A contract becomes a real bid prospect only when the solicitation is active, your company meets every mandatory condition, and the submission deadline has not passed.

No guarantee of funds, eligibility, award, or payment. Never pay a recovery company to search the official public database. For assignments, referral fees, or claims filed on behalf of another owner, obtain legal advice and follow the applicable state rules.
